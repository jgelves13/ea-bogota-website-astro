Types of Events

What kind of events do we organize?

Talks

We invite experts and professionals to share their knowledge about cause areas, impactful careers, program evaluation, and more. 60-90 minute sessions with Q&A.

Reading Groups

Discussions on key EA books and articles. From "Doing Good Better" to impact evaluation papers. Accessible format for beginners and experts alike.

Workshops

Interactive sessions where participants apply EA tools to their own decisions: career planning, donation evaluation, theory of change, and more.

Social Meetups

Informal spaces to meet other community members, share ideas, and build connections. Because impact is also built in community.

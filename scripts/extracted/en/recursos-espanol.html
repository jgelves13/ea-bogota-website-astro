Spanish resources

Websites, newsletters, blogs and groups about effective altruism in Spanish and across Latin America. If you're just starting out, this is the best entry point in your own language.

Libraries, portals and newsletters covering effective altruism, effective giving, global priorities and catastrophic risks in Spanish.

EA in Latin America

Effective altruism chapters and university groups active in the region. Many run open events, reading programs and local meetups.

AE Argentina
Argentine EA chapter with meetups in Buenos Aires and an AI safety course.
AE Chile
Chilean EA chapter. Host of EA Summit Santiago.
AE Uniandes
University EA group at Universidad de los Andes, Colombia.
AE Brasil
Brazilian EA community with programs, courses, and events like EAGx São Paulo.
AE Querétaro
EA group in Querétaro, Mexico.
EA UPY
University group at Universidad Politécnica de Yucatán focused on impact technologies and AI safety.
